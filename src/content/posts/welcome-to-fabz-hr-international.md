We are pleased to launch the new website for **FABZ HR International Inc.**, registered in Toronto, Canada.

FABZ HR helps organizations build strong teams and professionals pursue meaningful careers. On the new site you can explore our four services:

- **Recruitment & Executive Search** — thoughtful sourcing and selection for specialist, professional and leadership roles.
- **Fractional HR** — senior, hands-on HR support without a full-time hire.
- **Global Mobility** — people-focused support for cross-border moves.
- **Career Services** — resume and LinkedIn updates, interview preparation and career coaching.

## New: a free HR Health Check

Our [free HR Health Check](/hr-health-check) is a short, 5-minute questionnaire covering six essential areas of HR. It is a quick way to see where your people practices stand and what to prioritize.

## Book a consultation online

You can now [book a free consultation](/book-demo) directly from the website and receive your meeting invitation by email.

FABZ HR International Inc. holds an Approved Ontario Company Recruiter Licence. We look forward to working with you.
